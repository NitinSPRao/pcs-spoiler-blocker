PCS Spoiler Blocker

Chrome Extension for procyclingstats.com

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device.

PCS Spoiler Blocker collects no personal data, transmits nothing to external servers, and requires no account. Everything the extension stores lives locally in your browser.

What we store

  • Which races you have revealed — stored locally so results stay visible when you navigate back to the page.
  • The current calendar date — used solely to auto-reset spoiler blocks each new day.

What we don't do

  • No personal information is collected — ever.
  • No data is sent to any server, third party, or analytics service.
  • No cookies. No tracking. No fingerprinting.
  • Storage is never read by anyone other than the extension itself, on your device.

Permissions explained

  • storage — saves your revealed-race state locally. Nothing leaves your device.
  • activeTab — checks the current tab URL to show accurate status in the popup. Tab content is not read or recorded.
  • procyclingstats.com host access — required to inject the spoiler overlay before the page renders. Scoped exclusively to this domain.
