Data & Privacy

No data collected.
No data sent.
Full stop.

PCS Spoiler Blocker runs entirely on your device. It reads nothing about you, stores nothing about you, and communicates with no external server. The only thing it remembers is which race results you chose to reveal.

Local Storage

What gets stored

  • → Which races you have revealed today — so results stay visible when you navigate back to the page.
  • → Today's calendar date — used solely to auto-reset spoiler blocks each morning. Nothing else.
Data Practices

What we never do

  • × Collect personal information of any kind.
  • × Send data to any server, analytics service, or third party.
  • × Use cookies, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting.
  • × Read your browsing history or any page content beyond procyclingstats.com.
Permissions

Why each permission exists

storage Saves your revealed-race state locally in Chrome. Data never leaves your device.
activeTab Checks the URL of the active tab to show accurate status in the popup. No page content is read or recorded.
procyclingstats.com Required to inject the spoiler overlay before the page renders, preventing any flash of results. Scoped to this domain only.
